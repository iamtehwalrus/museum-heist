Museum heist

How to play

This is a recording of ten steps in a museum. At one step the lights go out, and someone swaps the painting in the Gallery for a fake. Work out who everyone is.

  • Move through time with the slider, the arrows or the numbered step buttons.
  • Tap a person to follow them. You see their trail and the rooms they have been in.
  • Note what you work out with the Guard, Visitor and Burglar buttons. Each tap moves a note from maybe to no to yes.
  • Send your report once every guard and the burglar are marked "yes". You get one report.

What each role can and cannot do is under Rules, further down. If you get stuck, the Hint button gives one step of reasoning at a time.

    Rules

    During the blackout, someone swapped the painting in the Gallery for a fake. Work out who everyone is, then report every guard and the burglar. You get one report.

    Each step, a person stays put or goes through one door. There are two ways in and out: the front door in the Lobby and the side door in the West Wing. Nothing can be seen during the blackout, but every rule still applies in the dark.

    • Guard: is never in the same room as another guard. Guards only arrive or leave at the shift change, which is a single step that you are not told. At least one guard is inside the museum at every step.
    • Visitor: never goes back into a room they have left. The Rotunda is the one exception: it can be crossed any number of times.
    • Burglar: is in the Gallery during the blackout. After the blackout, is never in a room with a guard. Is outside by the last step.

    Only the burglar has to be gone by the end. Guards and visitors may still be inside at the last step.

    Outside is not a room. Every level can be solved by reasoning alone, with no guessing.
