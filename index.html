How to Play

You're watching a playback of a museum heist. There's one burglar and a set number of guards and visitors. During the heist, the lights go out, and the burglar swaps the painting in the Gallery with a forgery.

Your goal is to identify the burglar and the guards and send your report. The burglar, guards, and visitors follow movement rules, and you must deduce who is who.

Movement Rules

During each step of the timeline, characters may stay still or move to a connected room.

  • Guard: A guard will never be in the same room as another guard. At an unknown time, there is a single shift change, and guards will only enter or leave the building at that time. Lastly, there is at least one guard in the museum at all times.
  • Visitor: A visitor never re-visits a room they have been to. One exception is the Rotunda, which they can enter multiple times.
  • Burglar: The burglar will always make it to the Gallery room for the blackout step. After the blackout, they will never be in the same room as a guard. By the end of the heist, the burglar will have left the museum.

Nothing can be seen during the blackout, but all rules still apply.

Instructions

  • Move through time with the numbered steps or the arrows. The Play button shows the night in a continuous playback.
  • Tap a person to follow them. You see their trail, and each room they have been to is shaded. Any room they have visited twice will be shaded a darker color. Tap them again or on an empty part of the map to de-select them.
  • Keep suspect notes with the Guard, Visitor, and Burglar buttons. Tap a role to rule it out for the selected person, and tap it again to bring it back. Your notes also show up as the colors on their clothes. Be sure to pay attention to how many total guards and visitors there are, as shown next to the Report button.
  • Send your report once you have identified the burglar and the guards. The report is made based on your suspect notes. Anyone not marked as a guard or the burglar is reported as a visitor. You only get one chance to send a report, so no guessing!
  • If you get stuck, Hint gives one step of the reasoning at a time.

Every game can be solved by reasoning alone, with no guessing. Outside is not considered a room. Guards and visitors may remain inside the museum at the end of the timeline.
